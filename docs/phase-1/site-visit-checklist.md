# Site-visit checklist: rental unit in Tashkent

Print this, or open it on your phone, for every unit you visit. Take a **laser distance meter** (≈ $20–40), a tape measure, a phone with a compass app, and a socket tester if possible. Photograph every wall straight-on plus each corner. Everything you measure goes straight into the app in Phase 2 (room width, depth, height, openings).

**Measure from finished surface to finished surface, in centimetres, and write down which wall you measured from.**

## 1. Shell geometry
- [ ] Length of every wall (go clockwise, starting from the entrance wall)
- [ ] Both diagonals (shows whether the room is truly rectangular)
- [ ] Ceiling height at several points. Height under beams, ducts and pipes
- [ ] Columns or pilasters: size and distance from two walls
- [ ] Steps, ramps, level changes, floor slope
- [ ] Wall construction (brick / concrete / plasterboard): affects drilling and mounting

## 2. Windows and shopfront
- [ ] Each window: width, height, sill height, distance from corners
- [ ] Which direction the shopfront faces (compass): affects sun glare and cooling
- [ ] Opening type, security shutters, existing blinds
- [ ] Rules for window graphics and stickers

## 3. Doors and access
- [ ] Entrance door clear width, leaf width, swing direction, threshold height
- [ ] Number of exits. Is there a second / emergency exit?
- [ ] Step-free access from the pavement? Ramp possible?
- [ ] Other internal doors: width and swing
- [ ] Lock quality, alarm, CCTV, roller shutter

## 4. Electrical
- [ ] Location and count of existing sockets (mark on a sketch)
- [ ] Distribution board location, number of circuits, RCD/earth leakage protection present
- [ ] **Allocated power (kW)** in the lease or from the utility. Separate meter?
- [ ] Earthing present (socket tester)
- [ ] Existing lighting and switch positions
- [ ] Frequency and duration of power outages in the area (ask neighbours)

## 5. Internet and telecoms
- [ ] Which providers serve the building (fibre / GPON)? Ask neighbours about real speeds
- [ ] Where the cable enters. Distance to the planned IT corner
- [ ] Mobile signal inside (all operators)

## 6. Water and plumbing
- [ ] Water supply and drain in the unit or nearby? (needed only for a sink or restroom)
- [ ] Restroom: inside the unit / shared / none. If inside: its dimensions and door position
- [ ] Any pipes or risers crossing the space

## 7. Climate and ventilation
- [ ] Existing AC units (model, capacity, age, working condition). **A major budget factor**
- [ ] Where outdoor units are allowed (façade rules, landlord)
- [ ] Winter heating: central radiators (where, how many?), gas, or none
- [ ] Fresh-air ventilation or openable windows
- [ ] Signs of damp, mould or leaks (ceiling corners, under windows)

## 8. Fire and safety (verify requirements locally, no assumptions)
- [ ] Existing smoke detectors, extinguishers, emergency lighting, exit signs
- [ ] Ask the landlord which fire-safety documents and inspections apply to a commercial office
- [ ] Gas pipes or meters inside the unit

## 9. Landlord and legal
- [ ] Permitted use (office/commercial), and whether a travel agency is allowed
- [ ] What may be changed: painting, drilling, partitions, flooring, ceiling, signage
- [ ] Exterior sign size and lighting rules (landlord and municipal permit)
- [ ] Who pays for repairs, utilities, AC maintenance. Lease term, deposit, indexation
- [ ] Reinstatement obligation at the end of the lease (does everything have to be removed?)
- [ ] Opening hours or noise restrictions in the building

## 10. Location and client experience
- [ ] Foot traffic and visibility from the street, especially at night
- [ ] Client parking or public transport nearby
- [ ] Neighbouring businesses (noise, smells, competition)
- [ ] Safety of the street in the evening
- [ ] Photos: each wall, each corner, ceiling, floor, shopfront from across the street

## Quick sketch template
Draw the room outline, mark north, and number the walls 1–4 clockwise from the entrance. Mark: doors (with swing), windows, sockets (S), data (D), radiators (R), AC (AC), columns, pipes. Then photograph the sketch.
