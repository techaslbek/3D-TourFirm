### Cuts that bring it within budget while keeping a 10 % contingency (proposed)

| Change | Saving $ |
|---|---|
| Ceiling raft over the desks only (≈ 8 m² instead of 14.6 m²) | 200 |
| CNC-cut painted MDF lattice instead of carved hardwood | 150 |
| TV 43″ instead of 50″ | 130 |
| Tea counter from modular kitchen units | 80 |
| Visitor chairs at $55 instead of $70 | 60 |
| **Total saving** | **620** |

→ Subtotal **$9 040** + 10 % contingency **$904** = **$9 944**.

### Excluded from this budget (confirm whether your $10k covers them)
- **Air conditioning** (≈ 2 split units plus installation). If the unit has no AC, the budget is broken, so treat "AC already installed" as a selection criterion.
- Laptops or computers, phones, software, POS/payment terminal.
- Rent, deposit, agency fee, utilities connection, internet contract.
- Tour-operator / travel agency licensing and company registration costs.
- Delivery and assembly charges, design permit fees, cleaning after works.
